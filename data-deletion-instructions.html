Data Deletion Instructions for Is My Horse

Last updated: November 21, 2025

This page provides instructions on how to request the deletion of your account and all associated data from the Is My Horse application, in accordance with Facebook's Platform Terms.

How to Request Data Deletion

To request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated data, please send an email to jouke@studio-mv.nl with the following details:

  • Subject Line: Data Deletion Request
  • Body of the email: Please clearly state that you wish to delete your account and include the email address you used to register with Is My Horse. This is crucial for us to identify the correct account.

What Happens Next

Once we receive your request, we will begin the process of permanently deleting your account and all associated data from our systems. This includes:

  • Your user profile information (name, email address, etc.).
  • All horse profiles and dossiers you have created.
  • All user-generated content, such as timeline posts, photos, and videos.
  • Your associations with any stables or organizations within the app.

Timeframe

We will process your request and complete the data deletion within 30 days of receiving your email.

Confirmation

After your data has been permanently deleted, we will send a confirmation email to the address from which you sent the request.

Please note: Once your data is deleted, this action is irreversible. You will not be able to recover your account or any of its content.
