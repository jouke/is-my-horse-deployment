Privacy Policy for Is My Horse

Last updated: November 20, 2025

Introduction

Welcome to Is My Horse ("we," "our," "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have in relation to it when you use our mobile application and services (the "Services").

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us, information collected automatically, and information from third-party sources.

  • Personal Information You Disclose to Us: We collect personal information that you provide, such as your name and email address when you register for an account.
  • Information from Google: If you choose to register or log in to our Services using your Google account, we access certain information from your Google profile. Specifically, we access your email address, your public profile information (such as your name and profile picture), and your OpenID identifier. This information is used solely for authentication purposes to create and manage your account and is not shared with third parties, other than our authentication service provider, Supabase. Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Horse Profile and Dossier Information: You may provide detailed information about your horse(s), including but not limited to their name, breed, age, training logs, and medical history ("Horse Dossier"). This information is a core feature of the app and is stored and managed by you.
  • User-Generated Content: We collect content you create within the app, such as status updates, photos, videos, and other media that you post to a horse's profile.
  • Information Collected Automatically: We may automatically collect certain information when you visit or use our Services, such as your IP address, device and browser characteristics, and usage data.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for various purposes, including to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Provide, operate, and maintain our Services.
  • Enable social features, such as displaying horse profiles, updates, and media to other users.
  • Allow stable managers to view the profiles of people and horses associated with their stable.
  • Improve, personalize, and expand our Services.
  • Communicate with you, for example, for customer service or to provide you with updates about the Services.

3. Sharing Your Information

As a social application, certain information will be shared with other users to provide the Services.

  • With Other Users: Your profile information, horse profiles, and any content you post (updates, media) may be visible to other users of the app, depending on your future privacy settings. Stable managers will have access to the profiles of users and horses linked to their stable.
  • With Service Providers: We may share your information with third-party vendors and service providers that perform services for us, such as Supabase for database hosting and authentication.
  • Google User Data: We do not share your Google user data with any third parties, except as necessary to provide our authentication services through Supabase.
  • For Legal Reasons: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

4. Data Security and Retention

We implement security measures to protect your information but cannot guarantee absolute security. We will retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with the Services.

5. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete the personal information we hold about you. You can manage your account information from within your account settings in the application. To delete your account and associated data, please contact us using the information provided below.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Services are not intended for use by children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
